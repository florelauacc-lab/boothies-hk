🗣 Cantonese 101 Hong Kong speaks Cantonese, not Mandarin

"Mm-goi" thanks "Yum sing!" cheers "Ho sik!" delicious "My-dan" the bill, please "Yau lok!" stop the minibus "Leng lui / leng jai" pretty / handsome

🎵 Cantonese has 6 tones, so "si" can mean poem, history, time, try, market, a thing… or poo 💩. Choose wisely.
